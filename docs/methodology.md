# Methodology

1. Acquire and register public datasets.
2. Harmonize time, geography, commodity, and units.
3. Assess missingness, outliers, structural breaks, and consistency.
4. Construct lagged, rolling, seasonal, climate, production, arrival, and macroeconomic features.
5. Fit statistical baselines and machine-learning models.
6. Define and classify food-price shocks.
7. Evaluate using rolling-origin validation.
8. Explain predictions globally and locally.
9. Translate predicted price changes into HFASI.
10. Conduct scenario and sensitivity analysis.
